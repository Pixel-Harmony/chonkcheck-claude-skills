# Onboarding Copy Refinement - Format Example

> Shows expected format for copy refinements. Your actual refinements should follow this structure.

## Executive Summary

**Source**: Onboarding UX design (3-step flow)
**Tone**: Welcoming, reassuring, efficient
**Key Changes**: Removed jargon, added British personality, made CTAs specific

---

## Copy Refinement by Section

### Step 1: Welcome

| Element | Before | After | Rationale |
|---------|--------|-------|-----------|
| Heading | "Welcome to ChonkCheck!" | "Welcome! Let's get you sorted." | "sorted" is British, removes redundant app name |
| Subheading | "Let's personalize your tracking experience. This takes less than a minute." | "Quick setup—less than a minute. Promise." | Removed jargon, added self-aware "Promise" |
| Button | "Continue" | "Start →" | More specific for first step, arrow adds momentum |
| Tooltip | "This will be used throughout the app" | "We'll use this everywhere—recipes, diary, weight logs" | Specific examples instead of generic |

### Step 2: Body Profile

| Element | Before | After | Rationale |
|---------|--------|-------|-----------|
| Subheading | "...based on your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)" | "We use this to work out your daily calorie needs" | Removed acronym, "work out" is British |
| Tooltip (Sex) | "Used in BMR calculation formula" | "We use this for calorie calculations" | Removed technical acronym |
| Tooltip (Activity) | "Your typical weekly activity" | "How active are you most weeks?" | Question format more engaging |

### Step 3: Goals

| Element | Before | After | Rationale |
|---------|--------|-------|-----------|
| Subheading | "Choose your goal to get started" | "Pick your goal. You can always change it later." | "Pick" more casual, reassures user |
| TDEE Card | "Your Calculated TDEE" | "Your Daily Energy" | Removed acronym |
| Goal button | "(-0.5 kg/week)" | "(lose ~0.5 kg/week)" | Added verb for clarity, tilde shows approximation |
| Customize | "Customize Macros" | "Customise Macros" | British spelling |
| Final CTA | "Start Tracking" | "Start Tracking →" | Arrow for momentum |

## Localization

- ✅ British spellings: "Customise"
- ✅ British slang: "sorted", "work out"
- ✅ Metric units: kg, cm
- ✅ Contractions: "We'll", "You're"

---

## Key Principles Applied

1. **Conversational**: "Let's get you sorted" vs "Welcome to ChonkCheck!"
2. **Self-aware**: "Promise" acknowledges user skepticism
3. **Specific**: "recipes, diary, weight logs" vs "throughout the app"
4. **No jargon**: Removed TDEE, BMR acronyms
5. **Judgment-free**: All goals equally valid, approximations with ~
6. **British English**: Customise, sorted, work out
