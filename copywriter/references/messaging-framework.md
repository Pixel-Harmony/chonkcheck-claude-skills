# ChonkCheck Messaging Framework

Strategic guidance for structuring product messaging.

---

## Value Proposition

**Formula**: [User pain point] → [Our solution] → [Emotional benefit]

**ChonkCheck**:
- Pain: "endless menus, confusing databases, too many taps"
- Solution: "ChonkCheck keeps it simple"
- Benefit: "get back to the good stuff"

**One-liner**: "Spend less time logging, more time eating."

---

## Key Benefits

1. **Speed**: Log meals in seconds, not minutes
2. **Simplicity**: No endless databases, no confusing menus
3. **Judgment-free**: Track honestly without guilt
4. **Smart features**: Recipes, saved meals, barcode scanning, label reading

---

## Feature Messaging Structure

```
Headline: Short, punchy (5-8 words)
Body: Specific scenario (2-3 sentences)
CTA: Action-oriented, clear next step
```

### Examples

**Recipes**:
- Headline: Cook Twice? Log Once.
- Body: That lasagne you make every Sunday? Save it as a recipe and add the whole thing with one tap.
- CTA: Create Your First Recipe

**Barcode Scanning**:
- Headline: Scan your scran.
- Body: Point your camera at any barcode and it lands straight in your diary.
- CTA: Try Scanning Now

**Weight Tracking**:
- Headline: Watch the trend, not the wobble.
- Body: Log your weight and see your progress over time. Focus on direction, not day-to-day.
- CTA: Log Your Weight

**Saved Meals**:
- Headline: Same breakfast? Just tap.
- Body: Save your go-to combinations—porridge with banana, eggs on toast. Add them in seconds.
- CTA: Save a Meal

## Messaging by Context

| Context | Tone | Focus |
|---------|------|-------|
| Marketing | Playful, bold | Pain points, emotional benefits |
| Onboarding | Welcoming, reassuring | Quick setup, build trust |
| App UI | Friendly, efficient | Help complete tasks |
| Errors | Helpful, not punishing | Acknowledge + solution |

---

## Scenario-Based Writing

**Golden Rule**: Describe a specific scenario, not a generic feature.

| Generic (bad) | Scenario-based (good) |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Recipe management system allows you to save frequently consumed meals | That lasagne you make every Sunday? Save it once, add it in seconds |
| Barcode scanning enables efficient food database lookup | Point your camera at any barcode and it lands straight in your diary |
| Weight tracking with trend visualizations | Log your weight and watch the trend, not the wobble |

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Does it solve a user problem?
- [ ] Can I visualize the scenario?
- [ ] Is the benefit clear?
- [ ] Is it conversational? (read aloud test)
- [ ] Is it judgment-free?
- [ ] Is it British English?
- [ ] Would I click/act?
